Set up a WebDAV folder
  1. Copy the HTTPS WebDAV folder address from your storage provider. A normal sign-in page will not work.
  2. Enter your username. Use an app password when your provider offers one.
  3. Check the connection, then sync. A failed check explains what to change.

These WebDAV details are not saved by the app. The password clears after each check or sync attempt.
