The handoff

One visible route from shelf to reader.

No opaque library import. No account. Each stage says what it found and what will cross.

  1. 01

    Index what you own

    Choose a folder. EPUB package metadata, PDF properties, cover status, duplicates, and protected files are checked locally.

    Source untouched
  2. 02

    Make order device-safe

    Arrange reading queues and series. Numbered folders and filenames preserve order even when a reader ignores metadata.

    Preview first
  3. 03

    Transfer, verify, eject

    USB copies are staged and hashed before they replace anything. WebDAV stops cleanly on connection loss and retries safely.

    Disconnect-safe
  4. 04

    Bring the margins home

    Import reader notes, KOReader sidecars, or embedded PDF annotations. Export the whole set as readable Markdown.

    Never locked in
