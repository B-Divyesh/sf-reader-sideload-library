Plain-language policy · 30 August 2026

Privacy stays on your desk.

The app has no telemetry, advertising identifier, or cloud catalogue.

Data the app stores

The app stores your catalogue, collections, preferences, imported highlights, and optional license token on your computer.

Source book files are read for metadata and are not rewritten. WebDAV credentials stay in memory only during a transfer.

Transfers you request

USB copies go only to the folder you choose.

For WebDAV, selected books and folder names go to the server address you provide. That server’s privacy terms apply.

Existing Field licenses

Existing Field edition license checks use the Sociobot billing API. The app sends the token only when verification is needed.

New Field edition purchases are paused. This site does not receive card details.

Website and demo

This website sets no analytics or advertising cookies. Its service worker stores public files for offline visits.

The demo uses a separate browser storage key. Leaving the demo never copies sample data into your real app library.

Your control

Clear app storage or uninstall the app to remove its local data.

Book copies and Markdown exports remain where you placed them. Email privacy@sociobot.in with privacy questions.
